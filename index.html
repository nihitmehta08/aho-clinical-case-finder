Acquired Hypothalamic Obesity Case Finder

A medication-neutral previsit tool that identifies clinical features that may warrant clinician review and creates a structured HPI draft.

This MVP is a case-finding and documentation aid. It does not diagnose acquired hypothalamic obesity, estimate treatment response, or determine medication eligibility.

Patient questionnaire

Answer based on what happened after the brain condition, injury, surgery, or treatment. No answers are transmitted or stored.

1. Have you ever had any of the following? Select all that apply.
2. When did the brain condition, injury, surgery, or treatment occur?
3. Did your weight begin increasing quickly within about 12 months afterward?
4. Did the weight gain continue or remain difficult to reverse?
5. About how much weight did you gain afterward?
6. Since that event, have you noticed any of these hunger or eating changes?
7. Since that event, have you noticed any of these other changes?
8. Have you been told that you have any pituitary hormone problems?
9. Has an MRI or clinician identified injury or disease involving the hypothalamus?
10. Optional details for your healthcare team

Clinical case-finding output

Clinician review recommended
Core features present 0 of 3
Hunger-related features 0
Supportive features 0

Important: This output is not a diagnosis, validated probability, or medication recommendation. A clinician should verify the history, weight trajectory, BMI or BMI-SDS, imaging, endocrine status, alternative causes of weight gain, and relevant comorbidities.

MVP for clinical discussion and faculty review. No patient information is saved or transmitted.
